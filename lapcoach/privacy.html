Privacy Policy for the LapCoach app

Last updated: November 2026

In short

LapCoach collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

Data processed by the app

You enter swimmers’ names, training sessions, times and lengths. This data is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. It is never transmitted to us or to third parties, and we have no access to it.

If you enter names of children or other people, please make sure you are allowed to process this data for your training, for example under your club’s rules.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, the app’s data is part of that backup under the terms of Apple or your computer. LapCoach itself does not use iCloud sync.

Export and sharing

With “Export” you can create a CSV or PDF file and share it through the iOS share sheet. You alone decide where the file goes.

Deletion

You can delete sessions, runs and swimmers in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

This website

These pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server log files to deliver and secure the pages. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details, including retention, are in GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages use no cookies and no analytics.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the app transmits no data to us, we hold no data about your use of the app.
